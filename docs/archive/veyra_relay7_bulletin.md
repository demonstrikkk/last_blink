# Veyra Civic Archive — Relay 7 Incident Bulletin

Reference: VCA/RL7/03:14
Classification: Public record, preservation copy

## Summary

At district cycle 03:14, Relay 7 recorded an unaligned eight-minute window. Every
other civic clock in the Aster district performed a reset at the same moment. The
relay's own log continued through the window without a gap, which is the reason the
incident was preserved rather than deleted.

## Known sequence

1. 03:06 — relay carrier drifts outside its licensed band.
2. 03:14 — district clocks reset; Relay 7 remains on its internal clock.
3. 03:22 — the relay re-aligns to the civic network without an operator present.
4. 03:31 — a ward dispatch runner signs out at the desk for a Relay 7 destination.

The dispatch slip for the 03:31 sign-out is handwritten. It is not present in the
ward's electronic dispatch ledger. The archive keeps both copies, and the two
copies disagree about whether the courier was sealed and authorised.

## Second Settlement

The Second Settlement is the scheduled re-attribution of the Aster district after
an unattributed outage. Attribution is a legal act, not a repair: it decides which
office carries responsibility for the lost minutes.

The Settlement calendar is fixed. It does not wait for a complete investigation.

## Archivist note

The archive records that a claim was made. It does not certify that the claim is
true. Investigators should treat witness versions and derived correlations as
separate classes of record.
