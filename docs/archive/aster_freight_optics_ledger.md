# Freight and Optics Ledger — Aster Depot Notes

Reference: DEPOT/OPS/7
Classification: Operational, preservation copy

## Freight

Aster depot runs one sealed freight rotation per district cycle. The manifest is
signed at the depot, countersigned at Relay Street, and reconciled against the
relay's own carrier history. A rotation that leaves the depot without a countersign
is recorded as an open movement and can be audited later without a warrant.

## Optical jumpers

Depot optical jumpers are calibration parts, not data parts. A jumper is keyed to
the carrier it was cut for; swapping jumpers between carriers produces a clean
signal with the wrong provenance. Depot technicians log each cut in the bench book.

An unauthorised jumper is therefore not a stolen object so much as a false
signature: the relay reports a carrier nobody dispatched.

## Locker practice

Depot lockers are frequency-checked, not key-checked. A locker opens to the carrier
tone recorded in the bench book. If the bench book and the locker disagree, the
bench book is the record that survives audit.

## Archivist note

Freight and optics records are the strongest material class in this archive. They
are created by equipment at the moment of the act, not by a person recounting it
afterwards. Correlate them before trusting a spoken account.
