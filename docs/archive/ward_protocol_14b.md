# Ward Preservation Protocol 14-B

Reference: WARD/PROT/14-B
Classification: Internal, preservation copy

## Scope

Protocol 14-B governs a preservation hold. A hold is opened when a settlement
cannot be attributed to a single office and material records are at risk of
routine deletion.

## Rules

- A hold freezes deletion. It does not freeze observation.
- A hold does not establish guilt, negligence or intent.
- A hold may be opened by a ward desk without the archive keeper's consent.
- A hold may be closed early by the office that opened it, provided the closure
  is logged against a named operator.
- Records created under a hold stay readable to the archive after closure.

## Named exceptions

Manual root tokens are issued to the district oversight channel inside the 03:14
window. Tokens are single-use and expire at the next 03:14 pass. The archive does
not store token values; it stores the fact that a token was granted and to whom.

## Archivist note

Protocol 14-B is frequently cited as if it proves wrongdoing. It proves only that
someone with authority chose to keep the record instead of deleting it.
